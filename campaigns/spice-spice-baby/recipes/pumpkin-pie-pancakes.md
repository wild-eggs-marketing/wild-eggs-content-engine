# Pumpkin Pie Pancakes, kitchen build

Source: kitchen recipe card, received 2026-09-10. Plate: 12" round.

| Qty | Ingredient |
| :---- | :---- |
| 12 oz | Pumpkin pancake batter |
| 4 oz | Salted vanilla caramel |
| 2 oz | Candied pecans |
| 1 oz | Whipped cream |
| to taste | Powdered sugar |
| to taste | Cinnamon |

## Procedure

1. Using pancake hopper, portion 2 × 6 oz pancakes onto oiled flat top. Allow
   batter to spread to form an 8" circle.
2. Cook pancakes on first side until set and bubbles begin to form, approx.
   3 to 4 minutes.
3. Flip and cook until golden brown and the centers are cooked through.
4. Remove from grill and stack in the center of a 12" round.
5. Ladle caramel sauce evenly over pancakes.
6. Spread pecans over top and place in window for service.
7. Garnish with a spiral of whipped cream in the center and dust with powdered
   sugar and cinnamon.

## Copy implications

- It is a **two-cake** plate spread to 8" on a 12" round, so it is wide, not tall. Drop
  "stacked tall" from any description.
- **Candied pecans mean this dish contains tree nuts.** The CMS allergen field
  and the site's allergen block both omit that. See `open-items.md`.
- Toppings are caramel, pecans, whipped cream, powdered sugar and cinnamon,
  which is what the photography has been showing all along.
