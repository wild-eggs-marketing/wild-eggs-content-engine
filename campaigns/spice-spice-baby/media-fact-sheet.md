## The items

### 1. Hot Honey Chicken Sandwich — $16.99

Served with french fries.

**Description:** Hand-breaded chicken breast, fried golden and tossed hot in our house-made hot honey sauce. Sweet heat in every bite, not just on top. Stacked on a jalapeño cornmeal waffle over a base of chipotle slaw, layered with candied jalapeño bacon and pepper jack. Finished with a charred jalapeño on each half. Served with fries, chipotle ranch, and chipotle slaw on the side.

Pairs perfectly with the Spicy Mary.

**Allergens:** Egg, Milk/Dairy, Wheat/Gluten

### 2. Sunrise Tostadas — $11.99

**Description:** Three crisp 6-inch corn tostadas piled with slow-cooked carnitas, cheddar jack, salsa verde, shredded lettuce and pico de gallo. Two slices of fresh avocado and four slices of fresh jalapeño on every one, finished with a verde crema drizzle.

Pairs well with the Spicy Mary.

**Allergens:** Milk/Dairy

### 3. Pumpkin Pie Pancakes — $13.49

**Description:** Real pumpkin, real pie spice with cinnamon, nutmeg, clove folded into a scratch-made batter and stacked tall. It's less "pumpkin spice everything" and more "grandma's pie, but you can eat it for breakfast."

**Allergens:** Egg, Milk/Dairy, Soy, Wheat/Gluten

### 4. Gluten-free Pumpkin Pie Pancakes — $13.49

**Description:** Same pumpkin spice, same tall stack, built on our gluten-free batter so nobody has to sit out the season. Ask your server for the full build. Our kitchens are not gluten-free environments.

**Allergens:** Egg, Milk/Dairy

### 5. Spicy Mary — $12.99

**Description:** House vodka, Zing Zang Bloody Mary mix, pickled jalapeño juice and a hit of Tabasco, with a Tajín and brown sugar rim. Topped with a charred jalapeño and a strip of candied jalapeño bacon. Built for the Hot Honey Chicken Sandwich.

**Allergens:** Soy (Bloody Mary mix). Garnish contains pork. Contains alcohol — 21+ with valid ID.

### 6. Caramel Apple Spice Mimosa — $9.99

**Description:** Sparkling wine, spiced apple and caramel. The mimosa went to the orchard.

**Allergens:** Sulfites. Contains alcohol — 21+ with valid ID.

### 7. The Wild Pumpkin — $7.99 classic, $10.99 spiked

**Description:** Pumpkin, spice and espresso, made the way a pumpkin latte should be. Keep it classic, or add the spike and let the morning run a little long.

**Allergens:** Milk/Dairy. Spiked version contains alcohol — 21+ with valid ID.

## Availability and fine print

  - **On sale:** September 30 through November 24, 2026.
  - **Where:** All Wild Eggs locations.
  - **Alcohol:** The Spicy Mary, the Caramel Apple Spice Mimosa and the spiked Wild Pumpkin require valid ID, 21 or older. The Bowling Green location does not serve alcohol.
  - **Allergens:** Per-item allergens are listed above. Wild Eggs kitchens are not gluten-free environments, and guests with any allergy should tell their server before ordering.
  - **Nutrition:** Full nutrition information for these seasonal items is in progress and available on request once published.

## About Wild Eggs

Wild Eggs was hatched in Louisville in 2007 from a kitchen table. The idea was simple: scratch-made food, real hospitality, the kind of breakfast worth getting out of bed for. Nearly two decades later, 19+ locations across Kentucky, Indiana, and Ohio are still cracking eggs to order every morning.
