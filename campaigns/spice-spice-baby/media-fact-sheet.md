## The items

### 1. Hot Honey Chicken Sandwich — $16.99

**Description (quotable):** Buttermilk chicken, fried and stacked on a jalapeño cornmeal waffle we make in house. Hot honey, candied jalapeño bacon, pepper jack, and a chipotle slaw to cool it back down. Served with fries and chipotle ranch.

**What's in it:** Chicken breast marinated in buttermilk and Frank's RedHot, hand-breaded and fried. Jalapeño cornmeal waffle made from scratch in house, used in place of a bun. Hot honey (Frank's RedHot, clover honey, real butter). Two strips of bacon candied in brown sugar and fresh jalapeño. Two slices of pepper jack. Chipotle slaw. A charred jalapeño skewered on each half — ask your server to hold it if you'd rather not. Fries and chipotle ranch on the side.

**Suggested pairing:** The Spicy Mary.

### 2. Sunrise Tostadas — $11.99

**Description (quotable):** Three crisp corn tostadas piled with carnitas, cheddar jack, salsa verde, lettuce, pico, fresh avocado and jalapeño, finished with a verde crema drizzle.

**What's in it:** Three 6-inch crisp corn shells. Each one gets slow-cooked carnitas, cheddar jack, salsa verde, shredded lettuce and pico de gallo, plus two slices of fresh avocado and four slices of fresh jalapeño. The whole plate is finished with verde crema — the kitchen's salsa verde folded into sour cream — so the heat and the cool arrive in the same bite.

**Suggested pairing:** A Spicy Margarita. Yes, at breakfast.

### 3. Pumpkin Pie Pancakes — $13.49

**Description (quotable):** Pumpkin pie is a dessert. We disagree. Our scratch hotcakes, pumpkin-spiced and stacked tall, so the pie finally shows up at the right meal.

**What's in it:** Wild Eggs' scratch-made hotcake batter, pumpkin-spiced, stacked tall and finished as pictured. Exact topping specification available on request.

**Suggested pairing:** The Wild Pumpkin latte.

### 4. Gluten-Free Pumpkin Pie Pancakes — $13.49

**Description (quotable):** Gluten-free pumpkin cakes with all the spice and none of the wheat. The same fall stack as the rest of the table, built on our gluten-free batter so nobody sits out the season.

**What's in it:** The same pumpkin-spiced stack, made with Wild Eggs' gluten-free batter. Please note: our kitchens are not gluten-free environments. Guests should tell their server about any allergy before ordering.

### 5. Caramel Apple Spice Mimosa — $9.99

**Description (quotable):** Sparkling wine, spiced apple and caramel. The mimosa went to the orchard. It's the one you order when orange juice feels like summer.

**What's in it:** Sparkling wine with caramel apple and fall spice, garnished with a dried apple slice. Contains alcohol — 21+ with valid ID.

### 6. The Wild Pumpkin — $7.99 (spiked $10.99)

**Description (quotable):** Pumpkin, spice and espresso, made the way a pumpkin latte should be. Keep it classic for $7.99, or add the spike for $10.99 and let the morning run a little long.

**What's in it:** Espresso, steamed milk, pumpkin and fall spice, topped with whipped cream and a spice dusting. Available spiked for $10.99 — 21+ with valid ID.

### 7. The Spicy Mary — price confirming

**Description (quotable):** A Bloody Mary that came to work. Tajín and brown sugar on the rim, a charred jalapeño and a strip of candied jalapeño bacon on top.

**What's in it:** House vodka, pickled jalapeño juice, Tabasco and Zing Zang Bloody Mary mix, served in a glass rimmed with Tajín and brown sugar. Garnished with one charred jalapeño and one strip of candied jalapeño bacon. Contains alcohol — 21+ with valid ID.
